# Original asset reference

This contact sheet shows representative artwork extracted from the supplied Backyard Monsters source. **It is not a screenshot from Unreal Engine and is not evidence of a compiled or play-tested game.**

Building thumbnails combine original layers at their source offsets, with the first animation frame. Monster thumbnails show one facing/state from their original sprite sheets. Sizes here are for comparison; they do not represent final in-game camera scale.
